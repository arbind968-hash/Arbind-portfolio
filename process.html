How I Work

A Simple, Client-Focused Process

A clear 5-step workflow designed to take your data from raw spreadsheet to reliable business deliverable.

01

01 — Understand & Scope

I review your requirements, data sources, expected output, and business goals to define a clear project scope.

Key Milestone: Clear requirements, input data, and expected deliverables.
02

02 — Inspect & Audit

I examine the data for missing values, duplicates, inconsistent formats, incorrect data types, and other quality issues.

Key Milestone: A data-quality overview identifying the main issues before processing.
03

03 — Process & Analyze

I clean, transform, and analyze the data using appropriate tools and workflows. Where useful, I build automated processing scripts, SQL queries, or interactive visualizations.

Key Milestone: Clean, structured data and analysis-ready outputs.
04

04 — Validate & Deliver

I validate the processed data, check important fields and results, and prepare the agreed deliverables with clear documentation.

Key Milestone: Validated datasets, reports, dashboards, or automation tools ready for use.
05

05 — Refine & Support

I review the final output and make reasonable adjustments based on the agreed requirements.

Key Milestone: Final deliverables aligned with the project scope.

How your data is protected and how project work is managed.

Client datasets are handled securely in isolated environments and are never shared. Files can be permanently deleted after project completion.

Clear Turnaround Timelines

Projects have agreed deadlines upfront, with regular milestone updates so you always know the exact status of your deliverable.

Transparent Deliverables

All deliverables include clear summaries explaining what was processed, cleaned, or built so your team can use the results immediately.
