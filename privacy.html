Privacy & Client Data Policy

1. Data Confidentiality & Protection

All client datasets, spreadsheets, transactional logs, credentials, and business operational information shared during inquiries or project execution are held under strict confidentiality. Client datasets are never shared, sold, rented, or distributed to third parties.

2. Local & Secure Processing

Datasets provided for cleaning, transformation, automation, or analysis are processed within isolated, secure local computing environments. Upon project sign-off and confirmation, raw data files can be permanently scrubbed from local storage upon request.

3. Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)

I am happy to review and sign mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to receiving proprietary business records or internal spreadsheet files.

4. Website Information Collection

Information submitted through the project inquiry form (name, email, project scope) is solely used to communicate with you regarding your request. We do not use intrusive tracking cookies or sell marketing data.
