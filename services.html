Data Cleaning & Standardization

The Business Problem

Exported CRM records and vendor tables often arrive with duplicated customer rows, broken international phone formats, mixed date standards (MM/DD vs DD/MM), and missing mandatory fields that derail database ingestion.

Verified Deliverables

Standardized, error-checked dataset in `.xlsx` or `.csv` format + a complete line-item transformation log showing every modified, imputed, or purged record.

Scope of Work:

Exact & fuzzy deduplication on multiple composite keys
Handling missing/null values via domain-specific logic
ISO-8601 date, phone, and postal code normalization
Removing hidden non-ASCII characters & whitespace
Schema constraint & data type validation
Database-ready relational structuring
